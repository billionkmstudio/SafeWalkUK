SafeWalk UK data sources and limits

SafeWalk UK combines several public datasets. Being open about where the data comes from, and where it falls short, matters for a safety tool.

SourceUsed forLimits
data.police.ukStreet-level crimeMonthly and delayed; locations anonymised to nearby points; not available for Scotland
OpenStreetMapMap tiles, street dataCommunity-mapped; coverage varies by area
Overpass APICCTV, street lamps, safe spacesSparse mapping does not mean there is no lighting or CCTV
NominatimSearch and geocodingPublic usage policy applies
OSM foot router / OSRMWalking routesPublic servers with no service guarantee

Live data is never cached

Crime, routing, safe-space and live-location requests always go to the network. The app only caches its own shell and map libraries so it can open without signal, which avoids showing stale safety information.

Privacy

Saved routes are stored in your own browser. Anonymous usage events (for example, a route search or share) are collected with Google Analytics 4 to improve the app.
