Walking home at night in Manchester

Manchester has a lively city centre and nightlife, with residential areas, canals and parks close by. Comparing routes by time of night can help you pick the one that suits you.

What to consider

  • City centre and nightlife areas. Busy and well lit, but the atmosphere changes late at night.
  • Tram stops and stations. Check the final stretch between your stop and home.
  • Canal towpaths and parks. Attractive by day but often unlit and quiet after dark.
  • Quiet back streets. Shortcuts can mean fewer passers-by and less lighting.

How SafeWalk UK helps

SafeWalk UK compares walking options using recent street-level crime data for the Greater Manchester Police area from data.police.uk, plus lighting, CCTV and safe-space data from OpenStreetMap. Use the After Dark view to see how risk changes through the night.
